Who We Are

Down the Line (DTL) is a members' tennis community founded in 2026. We keep it simple: consistent Sunday-morning tennis, a friendly-but-competitive club ranking, quarterly tournaments, and an after-social culture — the meal after the match matters as much as the match.

We track every match and publish our own club rankings, run our own match scheduler, and organize tennis trips — from resort courts in Korea to Bali and Tokyo.

Where We Play

Achasan — Main Court

Our home for the second half of 2026. Every Sunday, 6–10 AM. East Seoul, next to the Han River trails.

Around the City

Segok (Gangnam), Naegok (Seocho), Jangchung (historic downtown courts), and Yangjae. We book by the season and the occasion.

Visiting Seoul?

Come play with us

If your club — or just you and a partner — are visiting Seoul, join a Sunday session. We'll arrange the court, balls, and the after-breakfast. If you're bringing a team, we'll set up a proper inter-club friendly: team format, scoreboard, photos, the whole thing.
