Down the Line plays friendlies with tennis clubs from anywhere in the world. Passing through Seoul with your team? We'll host you — court, format, scoreboard, and the after-social included. Want us to visit? Even better. One email starts it.

Propose a friendly About our club
Two Ways to Play

🇰🇷 We host you in Seoul

  • Sunday morning slot (6–10 AM) on our home court, or a custom booking
  • Team-match format: doubles lineups, live scoreboard, club ranking counted
  • Balls, court fees, and logistics arranged by us
  • After-social: proper Korean breakfast or BBQ with the club

✈️ We come to you

  • We travel — Bali and Tokyo are already on our map
  • Bring 4–12 players, all doubles, mixed levels welcome
  • Happy to combine with your club's regular session
  • We bring gifts. And we take the team photo very seriously
How It Works

01Say hello

Email us your club name, city, roster size, rough level (beginner–advanced), and candidate dates.

02We set the match

We agree on a format together — usually doubles round-robin or a team match to 6 games per rubber.

03Play & eat

Match day: play hard, swap pins and stories, and let the after-social run long. Results go on both clubs' records.

Good to Know
  • Levels: our members range from improver to strong intermediate (roughly NTRP 3.0–4.5). We'll match lineups so every rubber is a real match.
  • Format: doubles only on Sundays. Singles rubbers possible on custom bookings.
  • Language: English is fine — several members speak it, and the tennis speaks for itself.
  • Costs: each club covers its own travel; the host covers courts and balls.
Challenge us

Wherever your baseline is — Seoul, Tokyo, Bali, or beyond — we'd love to trade games with you. Write in English or Korean.
